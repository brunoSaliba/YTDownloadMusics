# Role — Retrospective

Capture learning after the change, not celebration.

Record:

- what assumptions were wrong;
- what caused rework or delay;
- defects/security issues found late and why;
- conventions or system facts discovered;
- what should become durable Memory Bank knowledge;
- process improvements that are specific and actionable.

Promote durable facts to `.memory-bank/`; do not leave important knowledge only in this task.
